Entrion ("the app", "we") is an Android voice assistant made by Pravat. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we handle

  • Account: your name, email address and profile picture when you sign in with Google. Used to create and identify your account.
  • Your conversations and memory: what you say or type to Entrion, the facts you ask it to remember, your tasks and routines. They are stored against your account so the assistant can remember you. Different accounts never see each other's data.
  • Voice and screen content: when you use the assistant, your voice, text and, when you ask it to operate or read your phone, the content of the screen are sent to the AI service that produces the answer. This happens only while you are using those features.
  • Permissions you grant: microphone, notifications, contacts, location, camera and files are used only for the feature you asked for.

Google user data and YouTube

If you choose "Connect YouTube", the app asks Google for one permission: to upload videos to your own YouTube channel (youtube.upload). We use it only when you ask Entrion to upload a video, to send that video file and the title, description and tags you gave. The access token stays on your device and is requested from Google each time; we do not store it on our servers, we do not read your YouTube data, and we do not share it with anyone.

Entrion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Accessibility service

If you turn on Entrion's Accessibility service, the app can read what is on the screen and tap or type for you, only to carry out a task you asked for. It does not run in the background to watch you.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed by the service providers needed to run the app (cloud storage and sign-in, and the AI service that creates answers). We share it otherwise only if the law requires.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete items inside the app, disconnect YouTube at any time (and remove access in your Google Account settings), or ask us to delete your account and data by emailing mdimam12120909@gmail.com.

Children

Entrion is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
