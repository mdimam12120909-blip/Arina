What Entrion does

  • Talks with you by voice in the language you use most.
  • Opens and operates apps on your Android phone when you ask, using Android Accessibility.
  • Researches topics on the web and keeps the answers for you.
  • Remembers what you tell it, per account, and reminds you of tasks and routines.
  • Optional: connect your YouTube channel so Entrion can upload a video, with the title, description and tags you ask for, to your own channel.

You stay in control

Entrion only acts when you ask. It asks before sensitive actions, and you can stop any task at any time. You choose which permissions and connections to allow, and you can remove them whenever you like.
